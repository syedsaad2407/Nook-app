GETTING IN

Three messages and you're in a room.

No account wizard, no permissions screen. Nook gets out of the way so the conversation can start.

start

Name the room

Pick who it's for — a trip, a house, a standing Tuesday call. The room is the group, not the other way around.

invite

Add people, not contacts

Send a link. Whoever opens it is in. No syncing your whole address book to find three people.

talk

It just sits there, quietly

No red badge counting up. Nook tells you when someone's waiting on a reply — not every time anyone types anything.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Built for rooms of five, not feeds of five thousand.

Every decision in Nook favors the small group over the broadcast.

01

No algorithm

Messages show up in the order they were sent. Nothing is reordered to keep you scrolling.

02

Rooms cap at twelve

Past twelve people, a chat stops being a conversation. Nook keeps every room small on purpose.

03

Quiet by default

Notifications only for messages that mention you or land in a room you've starred. Everything else waits.

04

Rooms can close

Finished planning the trip? Archive the room. It's still there, just not sitting in your list forever.
